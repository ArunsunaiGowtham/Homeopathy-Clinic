Through successive dilution and succussion (potentization), we awaken the remedy's energetic curative frequency while eliminating any chemical toxicity.

Two individuals with the same diagnosis will often receive completely different remedies based on their distinct emotional traits, thermals, and vitality.

Stimulating the Vital Force

Instead of forcing biochemical suppression, our remedies nudge the body's self-regulating intelligence (Vital Force) back toward homeostatic equilibrium.
