Clinical Care Partnership

To ensure optimal therapeutic results, our practice is grounded in mutual respect, clear communication, and time-tested classical Hahnemannian principles:

1. Classical Homeopathy

Care is individualized and constitutional. Remedies are selected to gently stimulate your organism's self-healing Vital Force over time.

2. Integrative Collaboration

Homeopathy works safely alongside conventional medicine. Never stop or adjust your prescribed conventional medications without consulting your primary doctor.

3. Remedy Handling

Take pellets sublingually into a clean mouth. Avoid eating, drinking coffee, or strong mints 20 minutes before/after. Store remedies away from strong aromas and direct heat.

4. 24-Hour Rescheduling

Sessions reserve 45 to 90 minutes of dedicated physician time. If you need to reschedule, please provide at least 24 hours' notice to avoid late fees.
