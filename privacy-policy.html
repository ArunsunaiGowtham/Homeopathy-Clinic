Privacy Policy

How AuraPure safeguards your personal health information and consultation records.

Effective Date: October 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

At AuraPure Homeopathy Clinic, your confidentiality is sacred. We treat all personal medical details, symptom profiles, and consultation discussions with the highest standard of professional privacy and digital security.

1. Information We Collect

Only information required for your constitutional care: basic contact details, health chronology, symptoms, and lifestyle modalities voluntarily shared during consultations.

2. How We Use It

Strictly to repertorize your case, formulate customized homeopathic remedies, and coordinate your appointments. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

3. Security & Confidentiality

All records are safeguarded with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Only your consulting physician and authorized dispensary staff have access.

4. Discreet Dispensary Delivery

Custom remedies are shipped in plain, unmarked packaging without diagnostic labels or condition names visible on outer packages.

You may request access to, correction of, or an electronic copy of your records at any time. For questions regarding your privacy, contact our clinic coordinator:
